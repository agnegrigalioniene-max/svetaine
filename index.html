What makes the in-Motion Pro different

Grips angled for your wrist

Left and right grips keep the wrist in its natural position, removing the bend that causes wrist pain and nerve strain. Most users adjust within the first day.

Spring assist

A spring in the lower post absorbs each step and returns the energy forward. Firm and controlled, not bouncy, and quieter than traditional crutch springs.

Tips that pivot

Sure Foot tips stay in full contact with the ground at any angle: hardwood, carpet, grass, gravel and uneven ground.

Folds in half

The underarm crutch folds without changing your height settings and fits an overhead bin, a car back seat or a closet.
